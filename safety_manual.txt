RAILWAY SAFETY AND COMPLIANCE MANUAL

SECTION 1: ROUTING PRIORITIES
1.1 Passenger Express trains (Priority 1) MUST always be given precedence over Freight trains (Priority 2), unless the Express train is experiencing an emergency.
1.2 In the event of a tie in priority, the train closest to its destination is given precedence.

SECTION 2: TRACK FAILURES
2.1 If a track is marked as "broken", no train may enter it.
2.2 The dispatcher must immediately find an alternative route using active tracks.

SECTION 3: SEVERE EMERGENCIES AND HAZMAT
3.1 HAZMAT SPILL: If a train carrying hazardous materials experiences a spill, the node where the spill occurred is completely quarantined.
3.2 HAZMAT SPILL PROTOCOL: All trains must be rerouted to avoid the quarantined node entirely. Any train within 1 step of the spill must halt immediately.
3.3 ENGINE FIRE: If a train reports an engine fire, it is immobilized. All other trains must be routed around the burning train.

SECTION 4: SPEED AND WEATHER
4.1 Heavy Snow: Speed limits on all tracks are halved.
4.2 High Winds: Freight trains must halt. Express trains may proceed at half speed.
